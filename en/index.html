HELP & ANSWERS / 03

RedotPay: frequently asked questions

Concise answers anchored in official documents, with a clear distinction between historical demos and current guidance.

?FAQ
Will I receive a $5 sign-up reward?+

RedotPay documents a USDs 5 welcome reward for eligible new users. It expires 30 days after receipt and cannot pay for card issuance. Check the offer shown in your app.

Can I use the welcome reward to pay for the card?+

No. The welcome reward is for eligible purchases after card activation, not virtual or physical card application fees.

Can I add referral code rs8k8 after registration?+

No. RedotPay says referral codes must be entered during registration and cannot be added to an existing account.

Are the 60% or 30% card discounts still active?+

These percentages came from 2024 tutorials. They are historical, not confirmed current promotions. Check active offers in the app.

Does RedotPay work with Google Play or PayPal?+

Linking depends on platform, region and account details. The videos document both a successful test and a later Google Play failure in 2024; neither guarantees today’s result.

Can I deposit as little as $1?+

A 2024 video showed this scenario. Minimums, networks and fees change. Confirm the app’s deposit page and the sending platform before transferring funds.

What does RedotPay Pro include?+

RedotPay officially documents monthly and annual memberships with conditional cashback and card benefits. Read the current Pro terms and check regional availability.
